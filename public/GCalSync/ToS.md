## 2. Description of Service

GCalSync is a Google Workspace Add-on that syncs events from a source Google Calendar to your primary Google Calendar as blocked time entries. The Add-on runs entirely within Google Apps Script and does not transmit data to any external servers.

## 3. Requirements

- A Google Workspace or Google account with Google Calendar
- The source calendar must be shared with or accessible to the account running the Add-on

## 4. Data Handling

- The Add-on reads events from your source calendar and creates corresponding events on your primary calendar
- Configuration is stored in your Google Apps Script User Properties
- No data is collected, stored, or transmitted outside of your Google account
